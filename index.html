Rights. Every text on this site is in the United States public domain: the original works by age (Hobbes 1651, Boole 1854, and the further editions named on the method page), and, where a public-domain English translation is lacking, this site supplies its own working translations, dedicated to the public domain. The site takes its name from Leibniz's proposal that disputants should one day settle arguments by computation: Calculemus — let us calculate. It ends, deliberately and of legal necessity, at the threshold of Turing, whose papers of 1936 and 1950 remain in copyright.
